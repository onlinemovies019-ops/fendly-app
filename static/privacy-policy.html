Fendly Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Fendly is operated by the developer of the Fendly app. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to info.fendly@gmail.com.

Information handled by Fendly

  • Account and profile: Firebase account identifier, username, name, email address, phone number, state, city, and profile photo when you provide them.
  • Lost-and-found reports: report title, description, category, date, location (which may be precise), and item photos you submit. Reports are used to operate the service, show full reports to authorized administrators, and identify possible matches. Potential match details are available only to authorized administrators.
  • Location: with permission, the app can use foreground device location to help add a location to a report. Fendly does not request background-location permission or continuously track location.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token and notification delivery/read status may be stored. Match notifications are created only after an administrator manually verifies a potential match.
  • Payments: subscription status and a payment transaction identifier are stored by Fendly. Payment details are processed by Razorpay; Fendly does not receive or store your full card number or payment credentials through the app.
  • Private reports: lost-and-found reports and their images are visible to the report owner and authorized Fendly administrators. Other users do not receive report details or potential-match previews.
  • Pet adoption: adoption listings, pet descriptions, city, and listing photos are public. If you apply, Fendly stores your home type, pet experience, and contact phone. The lister can review applications; after approval, the applicant can see the lister's verified phone number when one is available.

How information is used and shared

Fendly uses this information to authenticate accounts, maintain profiles and reports, support lost-item matching, moderate report text and images, translate reports when enabled, send notifications, process subscriptions, and respond to privacy requests. Authorized Fendly administrators can access report details, including report locations, to help coordinate recovery. Potential matches are visible only to authorized administrators. A verified-match notification goes only to the owner of the lost report and does not include either user's contact details. When an administrator opens a map link, the selected location is passed to the mapping application or service.

Depending on the services enabled for the deployment, information is processed by service providers that support Fendly, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging; the configured database and image-storage provider (including Supabase Storage or the Fendly API host); Resend for email verification codes; Fast2SMS for SMS verification; Razorpay for payments; and Brevo for administrator email alerts. Report text and submitted report images are sent to OpenAI for safety moderation before storage. When configured, report text may also be sent to OpenAI or Google Gemini for translation. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Fendly does not automatically publish Lost/Theft or Found reports to Facebook, Instagram, or other social platforms. An owner may choose to generate a community poster or alert card and share it using another app; information in materials or messages the owner shares is then subject to the recipient platform's terms and visibility settings. If an owner opts in to sharing a reunion story, authorized administrators may review its category, city, and a temporary photo link and may manually share an approved story outside Fendly. This consent is optional and defaults to off. Fendly does not automatically publish reunion stories to social media.

When creating a public pet-adoption listing, a lister may separately opt in to publishing a framed graphic made from its first pet photo and a caption with the pet name, species, age, city, description, and Fendly app link to Fendly's configured Facebook Page and Instagram account. This is optional and applies only to adoption listings. Meta receives the submitted publication content under its terms and privacy policy; Fendly stores returned publication IDs and posting status. Fendly does not receive or store applicants' private details on those pages unless the lister includes them in the public listing description. Administrators can delete an adoption listing from the Admin Workspace, which first attempts to remove its recorded Facebook and Instagram posts. If Meta does not confirm removal, the Fendly listing is kept so an administrator can retry.

Fendly does not collect or use device IMEIs for lost reports, vault records, or public verification. A one-time data update removes previously stored report hashes and IMEI values from vault records. The app does not use background location tracking.

Retention

SMS and email verification challenges expire after 10 minutes. Temporary verification rate-limit records use keyed digests rather than raw phone numbers or IP addresses and are cleaned up after their limit window. Service-provider backups may retain deleted data until their normal backup-retention cycle expires. Push notifications already delivered to a device cannot be recalled. Copies of materials that users share through other apps are subject to those platforms' policies.

When account deletion completes, Fendly deletes the account's profiles, lost-and-found reports, adoption listings and applications, report photos, adoption photos, generated adoption graphics, and community posters managed by Fendly, and related match alerts from active service stores. Images hosted outside services Fendly controls cannot be removed by Fendly; contact us if you need help identifying such an image.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the Fendly app from your profile by selecting Delete account. You can also submit a deletion request without the app on our account deletion request page. The request deletes your Firebase sign-in, profile information, profile document copies, notification records and token, username reservation, blocked-account settings, pending email-verification challenges, lost-and-found reports, adoption listings and applications, content reports submitted by the account or concerning its reports, and related match alerts. Fendly also attempts to delete report and profile photos stored by services it controls. Deleting an account does not automatically remove adoption posts already published to Facebook or Instagram. If you want those posts removed, contact Fendly before deleting your account so an administrator can locate and attempt to remove the recorded posts. Copies shared through other apps and Meta's own retention are subject to those platforms' controls and policies. Deletion can fail temporarily if a required service is unavailable; the app will tell you to retry rather than report success. Copies of materials previously shared by users through other apps are controlled by the receiving platforms and may need to be removed there.

Community content

Fendly checks report text and images for safety before they are stored and provides in-app options to report concerning reports and block their authors. Reports are private to their owner and authorized administrators. Administrators may remove content that violates the Community Guidelines.

Security and your choices

Fendly uses authenticated services and access controls intended to limit profile access to the account owner and report administration to authorized administrators. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You can choose whether to grant foreground location permission and whether to include photos or location in a report. Android app data is excluded from the device's backup and transfer rules.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Fendly's features or service providers change. For questions, correction requests, or help with deletion, email info.fendly@gmail.com.
