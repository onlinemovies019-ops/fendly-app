Fendly Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Fendly is operated by Rohan. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to info.fendly@gmail.com.

Information handled by Fendly

  • Account and profile: Firebase account identifier, username, name, email address, phone number, state, city, and profile photo when you provide them.
  • Lost-and-found reports: report title, description, category, date, location (which may be precise), and item photos you submit. Reports are used to operate the service, show full reports to authorized administrators, and identify possible matches. A limited preview (title, category, date, and report photo) may be shown to the owners of reports that the system identifies as a potential match.
  • Location: with permission, the app can use foreground device location to help add a location to a report. Fendly does not request background-location permission or continuously track location.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token and notification delivery/read status may be stored to send match notifications.
  • Payments: subscription status and a payment transaction identifier are stored by Fendly. Payment details are processed by Razorpay; Fendly does not receive or store your full card number or payment credentials through the app.

How information is used and shared

Fendly uses this information to authenticate accounts, maintain profiles and reports, support lost-item matching, moderate report text, translate reports when enabled, send notifications, process subscriptions, and respond to privacy requests. Authorized Fendly administrators can access report details, including report locations, to help coordinate recovery. Users receive only a limited preview of potential matches; free-text descriptions and precise locations are not included in that preview. When an administrator opens a map link, the selected location is passed to the mapping application or service.

Depending on the services enabled for the deployment, information is processed by service providers that support Fendly, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging; the configured database and image-storage provider (including Supabase Storage or the Fendly API host); Resend for email verification codes; 2Factor for SMS verification; Razorpay for payments; and Brevo for administrator email alerts. When configured, report text may be sent to OpenAI or Google Gemini for moderation, translation or text matching. Image matching may process report images locally or through the configured matching service, which may include Cloudinary. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Fendly does not use IMEI or serial numbers to associate devices with accounts. The app does not use background location tracking.

Retention

SMS and email verification challenges expire after 10 minutes. Temporary verification rate-limit records use keyed digests rather than raw phone numbers or IP addresses and are cleaned up after their limit window. Service-provider backups may retain deleted data until their normal backup-retention cycle expires. Push notifications already delivered to a device cannot be recalled.

When account deletion completes, Fendly deletes the account's profiles, lost-and-found reports, report photos managed by Fendly, and related match alerts from active service stores. Images hosted outside services Fendly controls cannot be removed by Fendly; contact us if you need help identifying such an image.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the Fendly app from your profile by selecting Delete account. You can also submit a deletion request without the app on our account deletion request page. The request deletes your Firebase sign-in, profile information, profile document copies, notification records and token, username reservation, pending email-verification challenges, lost-and-found reports, and related match alerts. Fendly also attempts to delete report and profile photos stored by services it controls. Deletion can fail temporarily if a required service is unavailable; the app will tell you to retry rather than report success.

Security and your choices

Fendly uses authenticated services and access controls intended to limit profile access to the account owner and report administration to authorized administrators. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You can choose whether to grant foreground location permission and whether to include photos or location in a report. Android app data is excluded from the device's backup and transfer rules.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Fendly's features or service providers change. For questions, correction requests, or help with deletion, email info.fendly@gmail.com.
