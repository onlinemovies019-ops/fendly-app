Fendly Community Guidelines

Fendly helps people reconnect with lost belongings, pets, and loved ones. Keep reports accurate, respectful, and focused on recovery.

Allowed reports

  • Submit truthful lost-and-found information that you are authorized to provide to Fendly.
  • Use photos and descriptions relevant to identifying or recovering the item, pet, or person.
  • Provide only contact and location information needed for recovery; report details are private to you and authorized Fendly administrators.

Not allowed

  • Threats, harassment, hate, bullying, sexual exploitation, or graphic content.
  • Spam, scams, impersonation, knowingly false reports, or content that promotes illegal activity.
  • Another person's passwords, financial details, private contact information, or other sensitive personal information.
  • Content you do not have permission to post, or content unrelated to Fendly's lost-and-found purpose.

Reporting and enforcement

Lost-and-found reports are visible to their owner and authorized Fendly administrators, not to other users. Administrators may review unverified reports and must verify evidence before confirming a match. Administrators may also remove fake or abusive reports. Owners can delete their own reports. Fendly does not automatically publish reports to social media. Reunion stories are shared outside Fendly only through manual administrator review when the owner has opted in.

Only share a generated community poster or alert card outside Fendly if you intend to disclose the information shown on it. Once shared, its audience and copies are controlled by the receiving app or service.

Do not use a Fendly report to publish sensitive information about a missing person. Contact local emergency services when someone may be in immediate danger.

For privacy and deletion information, see the Fendly Privacy Policy.
