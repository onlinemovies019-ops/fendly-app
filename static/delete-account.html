Delete your Fendly account

You can delete your account in the Fendly app from your profile. If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email info.fendly@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include your account email or username so we can locate and verify the account. Do not send your password, PIN, or verification code.

What deletion removes

After we verify your request, Fendly deletes the account's sign-in, profile data, lost-and-found reports, related match alerts, notification records, and images stored by services Fendly controls. Images hosted outside those services cannot be removed by Fendly. Provider backups may retain deleted data until their normal backup-retention cycle expires.

For details, see the Fendly Privacy Policy.
